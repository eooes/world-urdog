## What was wrong and what changed

`Animator._rot()` applied Euler deltas in **bone-local** space (`bone.quaternion.multiply(q)`). Because the detective rig has arbitrary Blender bone rolls (e.g. `pelvis_01` carries a ~98° X rest rotation, arms carry heavy roll), local +X was never "swing forward" — so walk, run, jump, and emotes all rotated limbs around wrong axes.

The rigged path in `src/player/Animator.js` now works in **character space** with a collect → flush structure:

1. **Constructor precompute** — after one `innerScene.updateWorldMatrix(true, true)`, each tracked bone stores `qParentRestRel = qRootWorld⁻¹ · qParentWorld` (parent rest orientation relative to the model root) plus its inverse and the existing rest quaternion. Root-relative means character turning (outer group yaw) can't skew the axes, and no world-space math runs per frame.
2. **`_rot(key,x,y,z)` accumulates** per-bone Euler deltas in a reused map instead of touching quaternions — multiple calls on the same bone in one frame (e.g. `spine3` lean + breathing during walk) now compose correctly.
3. **`_applyPose()` flush** at the end of the rigged update computes `bone.quaternion = (qParentRestRel⁻¹ · qEuler · qParentRestRel) · qRestLocal`, and restores plain rest quaternions for untouched bones (this replaces `_resetBones`).
4. **Sign constants** `AXIS_SIGN_X/Y/Z` sit at the top of the file, applied centrally in the flush. I verified the default sign empirically by parsing the GLB's rest pose with a throwaway node script (deleted afterward): the chain `Sketchfab_model(−90°X) · fbx(+90°X) · metarig.001(X→−X, Y→+Z, Z→+Y)` puts the character at **+Y up, facing −Z** in model-root space (foot bones point −Z). With that facing, a raw `+x` rotation swings a down-pointing thigh toward the facing direction — so all signs default to `1`, no flip needed.
5. **Debug aid** — the constructor emits one `console.debug` listing matched vs missing bone keys.

Amplitudes, frequencies, emote definitions, the static-model path, and the `new Animator(innerScene, type, bones)` signature are all untouched — `avatar.js:140` is the only construction site (LocalPlayer/RemotePlayer go through `buildAvatar`/`animateAvatar`), so no call-site changes were needed.
